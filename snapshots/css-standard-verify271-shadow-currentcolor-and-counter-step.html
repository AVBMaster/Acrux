b271 — a box-shadow that names no colour paints and computes in the element's own currentColor, fully opaque; the engine had a fixed rgba(0,0,0,0.31) in its place, so a red element's shadow came out translucent black. And a counter list says its step whether or not the page wrote one: counter-increment defaults a bare name to 1 (counter-reset and counter-set default to 0), so 'a 1 b' reads 'a 1 b 1'. The three coloured boxes above carry colourless shadows so the pixel baseline guards the currentColor paint.
