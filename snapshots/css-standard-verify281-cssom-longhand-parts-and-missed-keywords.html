b281 — the declaration block was asked how many declarations it holds and answered with the number of semicolons. The reference engine enumerates a shorthand as the longhands it carries: a block whose only write was 'margin: 1px 2px 3px 4px' has length 4 and answers item(1) with 'margin-top', and 'border' is seventeen parts (the four widths, then the four styles, then the four colours, then the five border-image parts), 'background' nine with no 'background-position' among them because it lists the two axes, 'mask' nine with its position axes under their legacy '-webkit-' names, 'font' nineteen. Removing one part breaks the shorthand apart in place: after removeProperty('margin-top') the block says the three sides it still has and 'margin' reads back as nothing. Writing a shorthand also takes the longhands it covers out of the block, which is why 'font-size: 16px' followed by 'font: bold 12px/1.5 Arial' leaves nineteen parts and one declaration in the text. And the fold runs the other way too: four parts that all exist again are ONE shorthand again, printed where the first of them stood, so a block written as four 'margin' edges — through four setProperty calls or straight out of the style attribute — says 'margin: 1px 2px 3px 4px', the three sides a removal left behind fold back the moment the fourth is written again, and a shorthand written first and given one of its parts afterwards is rewritten rather than doubled. Two families do not fold at all, both measured: 'columns' and 'font' stay as the longhands they were written as. Beside that, three words the 'text-decoration' shorthand had never learned ('blink', 'spelling-error', 'grammar-error'), a line list the engine split around a colour it should not have re-joined ('underline red overline'), an echo that said the style and colour a page never moved ('underline solid' is 'underline', and an error line says neither), a 'flex-flow' that took 'reverse' as a word, and a 'mask' that swallowed a colour the grammar has no place for, put a second geometry box in the origin's seat instead of the clip's, and then dropped its own composite, origin and mode from the computed value.
